Homepage Mail

Homepage Mail is a small personal tool that shows the number of unread emails, and the sender and subject of the most recent ones, on the owner's private home dashboard.

It is built and used by a single person for their own Gmail account. It is not a public service and has no other users.

  • Access is read-only (the gmail.readonly scope).
  • It reads only the sender and subject of recent unread messages, never message bodies or attachments.
  • Nothing is stored, sold or shared with anyone.
